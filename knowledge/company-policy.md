### 1. Purpose
This Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) Policy sets out the commitment of the Board of Directors of Hexamatics Servcomm Sdn Bhd and its subsidiaries (collectively, "Hexa Group" or "the Group") to embed a systematic, consistent, and effective approach to managing risk across all business operations, entities, and geographies.

The Group operates across Malaysia, Singapore, Indonesia, Philippines, Nepal, and the United States, providing HR outsourcing, workforce management, IT managed services, and consulting solutions. Given the multi-jurisdictional nature of our operations and the Group's progression towards listing on Bursa Malaysia's ACE Market, a robust ERM framework is essential to protect shareholder value, safeguard operations, and demonstrate governance readiness.

---

### 2. Scope of Application
This Policy applies to:
* All entities within the Hexa Group, including Hexamatics Servcomm Sdn Bhd (HSSB), Agensi Pekerjaan HexaHR Sdn Bhd (APHHRSB), Hexamatics Consulting Services Sdn Bhd (HCSSB), Hexamatics Singapore Pte Ltd (HSPL), P.T. Hexamatics Infotech (PTHIT), Hexamatics Nepal Pvt Ltd (HNPL), and Hexamatics Consulting, Inc. (HCI).
* All business functions including Finance, HR, Legal & Compliance, Business Development, IT, Operations, and Procurement.
* All personnel, including Board members, senior management, employees, contractors, and work-site employees (WSEs).
* All operational locations — Malaysia, Singapore, Indonesia, Philippines, Nepal, and the United States.

This Policy is effective upon approval by the Board of Directors and supersedes any prior risk management practices. Subsidiaries may establish supplementary procedures consistent with this Policy, subject to Group-level approval.

### 3. Board Commitment & ERM Policy Statement
The Board of Directors of Hexa Group is committed to fostering a risk-aware culture where risk management is integral to strategy-setting, decision-making, and day-to-day operations. We believe that effective ERM enables sustainable growth, protects stakeholder interests, and supports our vision of becoming a leading regional HR and IT services provider.

In fulfilling this commitment, the Board undertakes to:
* Approve and champion the ERM Framework and set the Group's risk appetite.
* Ensure adequate resources — including people, technology, and budget — are allocated for ERM activities.
* Integrate risk considerations into the Group's strategic planning and IPO readiness processes.
* Review ERM performance and the continued relevance of the Framework on at least an annual basis.
* Foster a culture of accountability, transparency, and continuous improvement in risk management.

#### 3.1 Core Values Underpinning ERM

| :--- | :--- |
| **Integrity** | All risk information is reported accurately and transparently to the Board and Management. |
| **Accountability** | Risk owners are clearly identified and held responsible for managing their respective risks. |

### 5. Risk Appetite & Risk Parameters

#### 5.1 Risk Appetite Statement
Hexa Group has a **low to moderate appetite** for risks that could:
* Result in regulatory penalties, fines, or suspension of licenses in any operating jurisdiction
* Cause harm to the health and safety of employees, WSEs, or clients
* Compromise the integrity of client data or personal data of employees
* Undermine the Group's IPO readiness and compliance with Bursa Malaysia ACE Market Listing Requirements (AMLR)
* Cause reputational damage or loss of client confidence

The Group has a **moderate appetite** for risks associated with:
* Business development and expansion into new markets or service lines
* Investment in technology to support HR and IT managed services delivery
* Competitive pricing and commercial decisions

#### 5.2 Risk Parameters (Impact and Likelihood Table)

**LIKELIHOOD SCALE:**

| Score | Rating | Description |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | Rare | Unlikely to occur; once in > 5 years |
| **2** | Unlikely | Could occur; once in 3–5 years |
| **3** | Possible | May occur; once in 1–3 years |
| **4** | Likely | Probable; once or more per year |
| **5** | Almost Certain | Expected to occur; multiple times per year |

**IMPACT SCALE:**

| Score | Rating | Financial (Group) | Regulatory | Reputation / Operations |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | Insignificant | < RM 10,000 | Minor non-compliance | Minimal disruption |
| **2** | Minor | RM 10K – RM 100K | Regulator inquiry | Short disruption, recoverable |
| **3** | Moderate | RM 100K – RM 500K | Formal warning / fine | Client complaints, negative press |
| **4** | Major | RM 500K – RM 2M | Significant penalty / suspension | Major client loss, IPO impact |
| **5** | Catastrophic | > RM 2M | License revocation / criminal liability | Severe reputational damage, business discontinuity |

#### 5.3 Risk Matrix
Risk Severity Score = Likelihood Score × Impact Score

| Likelihood \ Impact | Insignificant (1) | Minor (2) | Moderate (3) | Major (4) | Catastrophic (5) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Almost Certain (5)** | 5 – L | 10 – M | 15 – H | 20 – H | 25 – CR |
| **Likely (4)** | 4 – L | 8 – M | 12 – M | 16 – H | 20 – H |
| **Possible (3)** | 3 – L | 6 – L | 9 – M | 12 – M | 15 – H |
| **Unlikely (2)** | 2 – L | 4 – L | 6 – L | 8 – M | 10 – M |
| **Rare (1)** | 1 – L | 2 – L | 3 – L | 4 – L | 5 – L |

**Action Requirements:**

| Rating | Score Range | Required Action |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Critical (CR)** | 20 – 25 | Immediate escalation to Board; action plan within 1 month |
| **High (H)** | 12 – 19 | Escalate to Management Risk Committee; action plan within 3 months |
| **Medium (M)** | 5 – 11 | Managed by Department Heads; action plan within 6 months |
| **Low (L)** | 1 – 4 | Monitor; review at next annual risk assessment cycle |

### 6. Risk Assessment Process

#### 6.1 Risk Categories

| Risk Category | Examples Relevant to Hexa Group |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Strategic Risk** | Failure to execute IPO, loss of key clients, competitor pressure in HR outsourcing, inability to expand into new geographies |
| **Compliance & Regulatory Risk** | Non-compliance with AMLR, Employment Acts, PDPA/PDPL, BPJS, EPF, SOCSO, IRD filings across Malaysia, Indonesia, Nepal, Philippines |
| **Financial Risk** | FX exposure across MYR/SGD/IDR/PHP/NPR/USD, liquidity risk, bank covenant breaches, debtor default, late tax penalties |
| **Operational Risk** | Service delivery failures, payroll processing errors, loss of key personnel, WSE management issues in Indonesia and Nepal |
| **IT & Cybersecurity Risk** | Ransomware attacks, unauthorised Zoho/Dropbox access, data breaches, BYOD vulnerabilities, absence of firewall (HNPL) |
| **People & HR Risk** | Key person dependency, contract disputes, statutory non-compliance (CP22/CP22A, SSF, BPJS), onboarding control gaps |
| **Reputational Risk** | Anti-corruption failures, client data leakage, adverse media coverage, related party transaction disputes |
| **Health, Safety & Environment** | Workplace safety incidents, non-compliance with OSHA (Malaysia), Law No.1/1970 (Indonesia), RA 11058 (Philippines), Labour Act 2074 (Nepal) |

#### 6.2 Risk Identification
Risks shall be identified through:
* Annual facilitated risk workshops with Department Heads and Management.
* Review of past incidents, penalties, and near-misses.
* Regulatory environment scanning across all operating jurisdictions.
* Review of internal audit and ICR findings.
* Business context analysis (client concentration, geographic expansion, IPO milestones).
* Input from front-line employees via designated reporting channels.

#### 6.3 Risk Assessment Frequency

| Frequency | Trigger / Activity |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Annual** | Full Group-wide risk assessment; update Group Risk Register; Board review of risk appetite |
| **Semi-Annual** | Management Risk Committee review of risk register and KRIs; update on High and Critical risks |
| **Quarterly** | Compliance Officer monitors action plan progress; reviews KRIs and reports to Management |
| **Ad Hoc** | Triggered by new market entry, new service launch, regulatory change, material incident, or M&A activity |

#### 6.4 Risk Treatment Options

| Treatment | Description & Hexa Group Application |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Avoid** | Discontinue the activity giving rise to risk (e.g., exiting a market where regulatory compliance cannot be achieved). |
| **Reduce** | Implement controls to lower likelihood or impact (e.g., dual-approval for bank payments, firewall installation, statutory filing reminders). |
| **Transfer** | Shift risk to a third party (e.g., key man insurance for MD, Regional Director and Head of Finance; professional indemnity insurance). |
| **Accept** | Acknowledge and monitor residual risk within appetite, requiring documented approval. |

### 1. Objective & Scope

Applies to all entities (HSSB, APHHRSB, HCSSB, HSPL, PTHIT, HNPL, HCI) and all personnel across operating jurisdictions.

---

### 3. Key IT Governance Controls

#### 3.1 Password Standards
* **Length & Complexity:** Minimum 10 characters (12+ for admin accounts); must combine uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters.
* **Rotation & History:** Max password age of 90 days for standard accounts (60 days for admin); cannot reuse last 5 passwords.
* **Lockout & MFA:** Account locked after 5 failed attempts; MFA mandatory for email, Zoho, Dropbox, VPN, and financial/personal data systems.
* **Device Lock:** Automatic screen lock after max 5 minutes of inactivity.

#### 3.2 Antivirus & Firewall Controls
* **Endpoint Protection:** Active, IT-approved antivirus required on all devices; definitions updated daily; full scan weekly.
* **Firewalls:** Mandatory across all office locations (remediating HNPL gap); rules reviewed semi-annually; logs retained for 90 days.

#### 3.3 Patch Management Timelines

| Patch Category | Description | Maximum Time to Patch |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Critical / Security** | Actively exploited vulnerabilities or critical CVEs | Within 72 hours |
| **High Priority** | Important security patches without active exploitation | Within 7 days |
| **Standard / Routine** | General OS, application, and non-security updates | Within 30 days |
| **Firmware / Network** | Network infrastructure updates (routers, firewalls) | Within 30 days (scheduled window) |

#### 3.4 Backup & Recovery Standards
* **Cloud & Financial Data:** Zoho data backed up daily/continuously (12 months rolling, monthly exports kept 7 years). Payroll/HR data backed up daily (7-year retention).
* **Backup Protection:** Offsite/cloud separation; at least one copy kept in immutable/offline ("air-gapped") format to resist ransomware; encrypted at rest and in transit.
* **Recovery Targets:**
  * **Zoho Accounting / Payroll:** RTO = 4 hours, RPO = 24 hours.
  * **Email & Dropbox:** RTO = 8 hours, RPO = 24 hours.
* **Testing:** Quarterly test restorations; full annual DR simulation.

#### 3.5 IT Equipment Purchase & Disposal

**Procurement DoA Thresholds (USD Equivalent):**
* **Up to USD 1,000:** IT Lead approval
* **USD 1,001 – 5,000:** IT Lead + HOF approval
* **Above USD 5,000 (or bulk > USD 10,000):** HOF + CFO approval per Group Procurement DoA

**Disposal:** Devices must be returned to IT; storage media securely wiped using industry standards or physically destroyed; logged in Asset Disposal Register; e-waste processed via licensed recyclers.

---

### 4. Incident Escalation & Access Management

#### 4.1 Incident Severity & Escalation

| Severity | Description | Escalation & Response |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Critical** | Ransomware, large data exfiltration, core system compromise | Triage in 1h; inform MD/Compliance/HOF in 2h; notify Board in 24h; restore from clean backups. |
| **High** | Single device infected, account breach, credential phishing | Containment; inform MD/Compliance in 2h; post-incident review in 10 days. |
| **Medium / Low** | Suspicious logins, unclicked phishing emails | IT investigation and remediation. |

> **Principle:** Ransom demands shall not be paid without Board and law enforcement consultation. Data protection authorities (e.g., under PDPA, PDPL) must be notified where personal data is breached.

#### 4.2 User Access Management Framework
* **Access Request & Changes:** Principle of least privilege; separation of duties on financial tasks; requests must be formally logged via User Access Request forms.
* **Offboarding (Critical Control):** Access disabled **on or before** employee's last working day. Immediate termination requires access deactivation prior to or during the termination meeting.
* **Periodic Reviews:** Bi-annual access review across all systems; quarterly review for administrator/privileged accounts.

---

### 5. Remote Access, BYOD & Data Retention

#### 5.1 Remote Access & BYOD Rules
* **Remote Access:** Mandatory IT-approved VPN and MFA when connecting outside the corporate network; session timeout after 15 minutes of inactivity.
* **BYOD:** Device registration and Line Manager/IT approval required; mandatory PIN/password, encryption, and supported OS; employee must consent to remote wipe of business data. Jailbroken/rooted devices prohibited.

#### 5.2 Key Data Retention Windows
* **Financial & Payroll Records:** 7 years (statutory).
* **Personnel Files & Employment Contracts:** Duration of employment + 7 years post-termination.
* **Customer / Vendor Contracts:** 7 years post-contract expiry.
* **IT Security Logs:** Minimum 1 year (3 years post-investigation for incidents).
